DropCheck (Android)
1. Android Studio (Koala or newer) > Open > select this folder. Let Gradle sync.
2. Run on a physical phone (Android 8+; Android 14 supported).
3. In the app: set your costs, Save and start, allow "Display over other apps" and the screen capture prompt.
4. Open an Uber Eats offer and tap the floating € button.
Tune Analyzer.parse() if your offer screen reads differently.
